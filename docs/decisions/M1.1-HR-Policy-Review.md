## 1. Document purpose

HR-001 defines the employee lifecycle coordination needed for internal role
changes, transfers, and offboarding. It connects HR records and notifications
with manager business justification, IT access administration, Security
requirements, and Compliance evidence governance.

The policy applies to employees. Contractor access lifecycle requirements are
explicitly outside its scope (HR-001 §2). Its purpose is lifecycle coordination;
it does not provide deployment, platform administration, or incident response
procedures. This review evaluates the supplied synthetic draft, not a live
company policy or a functioning RAG application.

## 8. Implications for future RAG ingestion

- Preserve `document_id`, title, organization, department, policy owner,
  version, approval status, effective date, review cycle, classification, and
  language as metadata. Map existing labels without changing their meaning.
- Preserve source path and content hash alongside document version. Because
  this editorial change retains version 1.0, version alone cannot distinguish
  the pre-review and post-review text.
- Use heading-aware chunks with both the document and section identifiers.
  Keep conditions and exceptions with the rule they qualify; do not retrieve
  a deadline without its planned/urgent or practicability context.
- Cite the actual supporting section and document version. For example,
  transfer-notification answers should cite HR-001 v1.0 §4.2.
- Extract cross-document IDs as dependencies. Until companion files exist,
  record unresolved references rather than infer their content.
- Route questions about deployment authorization, MFA implementation,
  incident procedures, or exact retention periods to the appropriate future
  policy. HR-001 alone is insufficient evidence for those answers.
- Keep this review report separate from the authoritative policy corpus.
  Its proposals and open questions must not be retrieved as approved rules.
- Keep the corpus synthetic. Classification metadata is a retrieval input,
  not a substitute for future access controls.

These are design implications only. No parsing, chunking, embedding, or vector
storage was implemented.
