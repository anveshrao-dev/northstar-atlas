## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes procedures for managing employee role changes, internal transfers, and offboarding at Northstar Technologies. It ensures employee information remains accurate, access changes are initiated promptly, and departments coordinate during lifecycle events.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all employees undergoing internal department transfers, job title or responsibility changes, changes in employment status, or voluntary or involuntary termination. Contractor access lifecycle requirements are outside scope.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities
### 3.1 Human Resources
HR maintains employee employment and role information, records approved role changes, notifies IT Operations of employee lifecycle events, and retains employee lifecycle records according to applicable company policies.

### 3.2 Employee Manager
The employee's current and receiving managers confirm the role change, identify business access requirements, review previous-role access, and approve the business need for new access.

### 3.3 IT Operations
IT Operations implements access changes in accordance with **IT-001 — Identity & Access Management Policy**.

### 3.4 Information Security
Information Security reviews privileged or sensitive access requests according to **SEC-001 — Privileged Access & MFA Policy**.

### 3.5 Compliance
Compliance oversees retention and review of lifecycle-related access evidence according to **COMP-001 — Access Review & Audit Evidence Policy**.

## 4. Employee Role Change Procedure
### 4.1 Initiation
A role change must be approved by the receiving manager and recorded by HR before the transfer effective date. HR records employee identifier, previous and new departments and roles, effective date, receiving manager, and approval reference.

### 4.2 IT Notification
HR must notify IT Operations at least **two business days before** a planned internal transfer. For urgent transfers without advance notice, HR notifies IT as soon as the change is approved and identifies the request as urgent.

### 4.3 Existing Access Review
IT Operations must review previous-role access. Access no longer required must be removed no later than the end of the transfer's effective business day. Temporary exceptions must follow IT-001 and applicable security policies.

### 4.4 New Access Requests
New access must not be granted automatically based on job title. The receiving manager submits a documented request specifying systems and business justification. IT processes requests according to IT-001; privileged production access also follows SEC-001.

### 4.5 Completion
Administrative completion requires: (1) HR lifecycle record updated, (2) IT acknowledgment, (3) previous-role access reviewed, (4) access changes recorded, and (5) evidence retained according to COMP-001. Administrative completion does not itself authorize privileged access.

## 5. Employee Offboarding
### 5.1 Planned Offboarding
HR notifies IT at least two business days before planned departure, where practicable.

### 5.2 Access Termination
IT disables employee access no later than the approved termination effective time. For urgent involuntary termination, HR and IT coordinate disablement at the approved separation time.

### 5.3 Asset Return
The manager and IT coordinate company equipment return and record outstanding assets.

### 5.4 Evidence Retention
HR and IT retain relevant offboarding records according to COMP-001.

## 6. Exceptions
Notification timeline exceptions require documented reason, requesting manager, relevant approval, date/time, and mitigating actions where applicable. This HR policy does not independently authorize exceptions to privileged-access security controls.
